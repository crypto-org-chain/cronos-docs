# 🏆 Ranks & Benefits

Your Rank as an Ambassador comes with status badges and recognition on our official channels. The higher you move up in ranks, the more responsibility you can take on and the bigger your impact - and privileges - become. Rank reviews by the core team happen periodically and reflect your consistent contribution and impact.

* **🌱 Entry-level Ambassador** - Toolkit access, starter mission, light mentorship.
* **🔥 Envoy** - Priority missions, occasional swag, feature highlights.
* **🛡️ Sentinel** - Early previews, mentoring of Rookies and Envoys.
* **🏆 Elite** - Regional or track lead; co-host rights for official events & AMAs.
* **🧭 Council** - Public program steward; input on proposals/roadmap, selective travel support.
