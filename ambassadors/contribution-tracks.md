# 🏎️ Contribution Tracks

Choose one or multiple tracks as a Cronos Ambassador and positively impact the ecosystem by playing to your strengths. We’ve given some examples as to what each track might look like on a day-to-day basis.<br>

* **Advocacy & Education** - Share and explain Ult and network to your audience through content, threads, videos, or live sessions. Help people understand what it does and why it matters.
* **Community Building** - Grow and nurture active Cronos communities through  campaigns, events, or activations tailored to your region or community.
